User experience design is the process of creating products that provide meaningful and relevant experiences to users.

## What is UX Design?

UX design encompasses the entire journey a user takes when interacting with a product — from the first moment of awareness through purchase, use, and troubleshooting.

## Core Disciplines

- **User Research** — understanding who your users are and what they need
- **Information Architecture** — organizing content logically
- **Interaction Design** — defining how users interact with the product
- **Visual Design** — the look and feel

## Getting Started

Start by reading Don Norman's *The Design of Everyday Things*, then practice with free Figma projects and participate in design challenges on platforms like Designercize.
